# Knowledge Base Sources

## Primary References

1. **Nesse, W.D.** — *Introduction to Optical Mineralogy* (Oxford University Press; 4th ed.).
   Mineral-by-mineral optical data: colour, pleochroism, relief, birefringence,
   extinction, twinning, cleavage, distinguishing features.

2. **Raith, M.M., Raase, P., Reinhardt, J.** — *Guide to Thin Section Microscopy*
   (2nd ed., 2012; free PDF by the authors).
   Clear tables of optical properties and the Michel–Lévy chart.

3. **Deer, W.A., Howie, R.A., Zussman, J.** — *An Introduction to the Rock-Forming
   Minerals* (Mineralogical Society).
   Authoritative numeric ranges for refractive indices and birefringence.

4. **MacKenzie, W.S., Adams, A.E.** — *A Colour Atlas of Rocks and Minerals in
   Thin Section* (Manson Publishing).
   Visual appearance, habits, and photomicrographs.

## Online databases

- **mindat.org** — "Optical Data" section: refractive indices, birefringence δ,
  pleochroism, 2V angle.
- **webmineral.com** — Optical data for cross-checking numeric δ ranges.

## Verification

Every row in `minerals.csv` has been verified against at least two of the
above sources. The `source` column records which references were used.
`fine_aggregate` is derived from the project segmentation definition, not
from optical mineralogy references.
